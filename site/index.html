02 — how it works

sixty seconds.
zero code.

  1. 01

    say hi.

    Your name and a selfie — or your cat, or your OC. Your holo card shows up instantly. That's you already.

  2. 02

    throw in your favorites.

    Search any song, movie or book. Songs become records you can spin, movies go on your film reel, books line up on your shelf. Skip anything — it still looks good.

  3. 03

    answer weird little questions.

    While your space builds, we ask the fun stuff. Every answer makes it more you — no staring at a loading bar.

  4. 04

    drop the link.

    aura.my/you, with a share card that looks like you. Bio, group chat, story — wherever your people are.
