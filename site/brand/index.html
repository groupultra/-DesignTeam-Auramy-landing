01 · the idea

a corner, not a feed.

The internet got big and loud. Auramy gives you a small piece of it back: a page with your name on the door, your stuff inside, and your people dropping by. Every part of the brand points at one picture: a little world on a screen, and it's yours.

  • a place, not a profile

    Your page is somewhere friends go, poke things, play and leave a mark. It has an address: aura.my/you.

  • for your people, not your followers

    The group chat, the team, close friends. Small on purpose.

  • it moves into any screen

    Old desktop, new desktop, a game, a life sim. The screen changes. The smile stays.

02 · five constants

five things that never change.

Window chrome, buttons and UI type change with the screen Auramy lives in. These five come with it everywhere.

  1. the crayon smiley

    You, drawn by hand. A little lopsided, always yellow, always looking back at you.

    #f6ec22 face, #161a2e features. Never a perfect circle, never an emoji.

  2. the blue screen

    The screen is on. Auramy blue is the glow of your corner and the accent of every system it moves into.

    #2f5ef6 behind the smiley and on the main button. Never washed out.

  3. sky over grass

    Outside the window there's a world. Blue sky up, green grass down, room for anything in between.

    Horizon a little below the middle. A soft hill, never a hard line.

  4. ui mimicry

    We move into the screen you already know and borrow its windows, buttons and type. The smiley takes the system's logo spot.

    Mimic the system, never another brand's logo.

  5. aura.my/you

    your corner

    Every page has an address, a door and visitors. The person is the hero. Auramy stays small in the corner.

    Show the person first. The brand signs off, it doesn't headline.

05 · sky & grass

the wallpaper of your world.

Every Auramy screen has an outside: blue sky, a green hill, a few clouds drawn in pen. Your window sits on the horizon, like a house on a hill. It's the most direct way to say this is a place.

06 · ui mimicry

it wears your screen, keeps its smile.

Auramy doesn't bring its own operating system. It moves into the one you know and borrows its windows, buttons, type and navigation. Three things never move: the smiley takes the system's logo spot, Auramy blue becomes the system accent, and sky over grass becomes the wallpaper. Try it:
